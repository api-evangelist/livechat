You need to master typing skills in nearly every profession today, particularly in customer service, where agents rely on real-time communication with customers. The method you choose to type, touch typing or hunt and peck, will significantly affect your efficiency and accuracy. While the fastest touch typists can easily reach speeds of 120 words per minute (WPM), hunt and peck typists, on average, reach only around 27 WPM.
