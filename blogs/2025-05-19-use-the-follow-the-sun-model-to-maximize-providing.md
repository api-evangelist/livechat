As businesses expand across borders and time zones, the need for continuous customer support team operations has become more critical than ever. The Follow-the-Sun (FTS) model offers a practical solution by distributing work across globally dispersed teams, enabling around the clock productivity. Rather than pausing overnight, tasks move seamlessly from one region to another, accelerating delivery and improving responsiveness.
