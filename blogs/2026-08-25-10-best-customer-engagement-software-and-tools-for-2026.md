Customer engagement software is the set of tools a business uses to hold, route, and act on conversations with customers across channels — chat, email, messaging, and voice — and to measure what those conversations produce. Service-led engagement software sits in the support team's hands, not marketing's. That distinction matters more than it sounds, and it's the reason most shortlists in this category are useless.
