Providing excellent customer service is essential for any business, and live chat has become a key channel for connecting with customers quickly and directly. However, simply having live chat functionality is not enough to ensure it’s working effectively. To truly understand how well your live chat is performing and where improvements can be made, it’s important to look at live chat reports.
