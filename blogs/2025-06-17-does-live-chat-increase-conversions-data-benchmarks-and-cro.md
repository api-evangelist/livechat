Live chat can increase conversions when it resolves a question that would otherwise prevent a purchase or qualified inquiry. Product comparisons, pricing questions, and checkout problems are useful places to test it. But a higher conversion rate among chat users does not prove that chat caused the difference: those visitors may already be closer to buying.
