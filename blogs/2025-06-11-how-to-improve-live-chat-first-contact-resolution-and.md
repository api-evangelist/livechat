When 73% of customers say that valuing their time is the most important thing a company can do during a service interaction, it sends a loud message to support teams: speed alone isn’t enough, and resolution is what counts. Every time a customer is forced to follow up, repeat their issue, or get transferred to a different agent, trust erodes. What starts as a small inconvenience can quickly snowball into frustration, and ultimately churn.
