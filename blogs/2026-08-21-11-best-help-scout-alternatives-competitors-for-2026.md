Help Scout is a popular choice for teams that want a straightforward shared inbox, knowledge base, live chat, and customer support tools without the complexity of a large enterprise help desk. But it isn't the best fit for every team. You may need stronger real-time chat, more channels, deeper automation, a tighter CRM connection, ecommerce workflows, or simply a pricing model that matches the way your support team operates.
